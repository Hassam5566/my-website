About Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

Understanding how AI is transforming emergency medical response

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence such as decision-making, learning, and pattern recognition.

In healthcare, AI helps analyse large volumes of medical data quickly and accurately, supporting doctors and emergency teams in making faster decisions.

AI in Emergency Healthcare

AI plays a critical role in emergency healthcare systems by providing real-time insights and assisting in life-saving decisions.

  • ✔ Analysing patient symptoms instantly
  • ✔ Predicting medical emergencies early
  • ✔ Assisting doctors with diagnosis
  • ✔ Improving response time
